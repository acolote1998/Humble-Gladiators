Humble Gladiators

Humble Gladiators is an engaging turn-based combat game where you create and control unique gladiators, each with their own set of attributes that influence their performance in battle. Customize your gladiators by adjusting their Health, Speed, Strength, Dexterity, Luck, and more, and then take them into combat against a variety of challenging enemies.

You can create new gladiators and save them under your username for future battles. The game allows you to load previously created gladiators, so you can continue building your legacy. Track your victories on the "Podium," where you can see a ranking of the enemies you've defeated. The battle screen provides an immersive experience, where you’ll carefully choose your actions—whether it’s attacking, defending, or focusing—to outsmart your opponents and claim victory. Whether you’re battling for glory or working to build the perfect gladiator, Humble Gladiators offers endless strategic possibilities and challenges.

Gladiator Characteristics
Each gladiator in Humble Gladiators is a unique character defined by a set of core attributes that influence their performance in combat. These attributes affect everything from attack and defense capabilities to healing and resistance to debuffs. Below is a breakdown of the key characteristics and attributes of a gladiator:

Health (HP)
Description: Health points (HP) represent a gladiator’s vitality. This attribute indicates how much damage they can take before being defeated. Once a gladiator’s HP reaches zero, they are knocked out and can no longer participate in the battle.
Influences: HP is primarily affected by the gladiator’s Constitution.

Speed
Description: Speed determines the order of turns in battle. Gladiators with higher Speed act before those with lower Speed, making it crucial for executing strategic actions like attacking, healing, or defending before the opponent can react.
Influences: The higher a gladiator's Speed, the more likely they are to act first. If two gladiators have the same Speed, the one with lower HP will act first.

Constitution (HP)
Description: Constitution directly impacts a gladiator’s total Health (HP). Gladiators with higher Constitution have more HP, enabling them to endure more damage and stay in the fight longer.

Dexterity (Damage with Dexterity Weapons)
Description: Dexterity affects the effectiveness of ranged or finesse-based weapons, such as bows. It determines the damage dealt with weapons that rely on speed, precision, and agility, rather than raw power.

Strength (Damage with Strength Weapons)
Description: Strength is the key attribute for gladiators wielding heavy, powerful weapons like swords, axes, or maces. It influences the damage dealt by strength-based weapons, which focus on raw power rather than finesse.

Luck (Critical Hit Rate)
Description: Luck directly affects the chance of landing a Critical Hit (Crit). A Critical Hit deals significantly more damage than a regular hit and can dramatically change the course of a battle.
How it works: Luck influences the Critical Hit rate, which is the probability of landing a Crit. A higher Luck stat increases the chance of a Critical Hit.
Creation Process: When creating a new gladiator, we randomly determine their Luck within a specified range. We then flip a coin for each luck point—every heads adds an extra Luck point. We stop generating luck when the first tails is flipped.

Battle System Overview
The battle system in Humble Gladiators is a dynamic, turn-based system where players control a gladiator and face off against an enemy. Each turn, both the player and the enemy select an action, and the system resolves those actions based on the gladiators' attributes and chosen moves. The mechanics are designed to create an engaging and strategic experience, with an emphasis on decision-making, timing, and resource management.

Key Features
Actions: Each gladiator can perform one of three actions per turn: Attack, Defend, or Focus.

Attack: Deal damage to the opponent.
Defend: Reduce incoming damage and potentially heal the user, depending on the gladiator's state.
Focus: Increase the crit chance when attacking or the healing effect when defending in future actions.
Turn Resolution: At the start of each turn, the system determines the turn order based on the gladiators' Speed. If two gladiators have the same Speed, the one with lower maximum HP acts first.

Action Icons and Log: The system displays icons representing the chosen actions for both the hero and the enemy. A battle log tracks the actions taken and their outcomes.

Combat Resolution Flow
Action Selection:
On each turn, both the player and the enemy select their action. The player sets the hero's action (e.g., attack, defend, or focus), while the enemy's action is determined by the AI.

Turn Order Determination:
The battle system first determines the turn order based on each gladiator's Speed. The gladiator with the higher Speed (or, if equal, the one with lower HP) acts first.

Action Execution:
Once the turn order is set:

If the gladiator attacks, the system calculates the damage dealt to the opponent, factoring in whether the enemy is defending. Critical hits may also occur, dealing more damage. If the gladiator is focused, their crit chance increases. If they land a critical hit while focused, they lose the Focus status.
If the gladiator defends, the system reduces incoming damage if attacked and applies healing if not. If the defender is focused, they mitigate all normal attack damage or half of a critical hit's damage. If not attacked while focused, the healing effect is boosted. After defending while focused, the gladiator loses their Focus.
Focus does not directly affect the current turn, but it sets up future turns by increasing critical hit chance or healing capabilities.
Turn Completion:
At the end of each turn, the system updates the gladiators' health and checks for any status changes (such as losing Focus). The battle log is updated with the actions taken and their outcomes. The game then checks if the battle is still ongoing, and the turn count increases.

Victory Conditions:
The battle continues until one gladiator’s health reaches zero, signaling their defeat. The game then determines the winner. If both gladiators deliver a final blow at the same time, the gladiator with the higher Speed wins, as they strike first.

Summary of Turn-based Actions
Hero’s Turn: The hero selects an action (Attack, Defend, or Focus), determining how the turn unfolds—from damage dealt to healing or preparing for future actions.
Enemy’s Turn: The enemy AI chooses an action based on its strategy, which may involve attacking, defending, or focusing. If the hero’s HP is low, the enemy will prioritize attacking.
Conclusion
The battle system in Humble Gladiators is designed to be both strategic and engaging. Players must carefully consider their actions each turn, balancing offense, defense, and Focus to outsmart their opponents. Managing health and adapting strategies is key to success. With dynamic combat mechanics, every battle presents a new challenge, offering deep strategic gameplay for players who enjoy turn-based combat with rich, interactive elements.

Critical Hits
Critical hits introduce an element of randomness and excitement to combat. The chance of a Critical Hit is determined by the attacker’s Critical Rate, which is influenced by their Luck attribute. When a Critical Hit occurs, the damage is boosted by a Crit Multiplier. If the gladiator is focused, the crit chance is doubled.
